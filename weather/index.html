    About these numbers

    • Forecast values come from Open-Meteo, issued every hour for the coordinates of each capital. Observations come from the AEMET station linked to each capital, often at an airport or on the outskirts, so they can differ from the city centre.
    • The forecast error is the mean absolute difference between yesterday’s forecasts (made 1 to 6 hours ahead) and what the stations measured. It is recomputed every day by the warehouse.
    • AEMET publishes observations with some delay, so the last observation is usually one or two hours old. Its time is shown next to it.
    • Your browser downloads the snapshot directly from Hugging Face; Horizonte has no server. The warehouse code, schedule and data model are on GitHub.

    Horizonte is not an official service and issues no warnings. For official forecasts and warnings in Spain see AEMET and Meteoalarm.
